### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment throughout the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program was highly open, professional, and encouraging. Team members and mentors were consistently approachable and ready to support whenever technical roadblocks arose. The collaborative dynamic provided a great space to stay focused while building cloud-native solutions.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The technical guidance provided by the mentors was outstanding. Rather than simply giving direct answers, my mentor encouraged me to investigate root causes independently before offering architectural direction. The administrative team ensured smooth coordination, clear task timelines, and prompt access to necessary AWS learning materials and credits.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The assigned project tasks aligned perfectly with my academic field in Computer Science and Information Technology. Working on hardware-to-cloud integration (AWS IoT Core, MQTT), serverless backend development (AWS Lambda, Database modeling), and web hosting (AWS Amplify) bridge the gap between classroom theory and real-world system architecture.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
This program significantly accelerated my technical and professional growth. Beyond getting hands-on experience with core AWS services, I developed better disciplined work habits—such as structuring daily progress reports (Done / In Progress / Blockers), refining my problem-solving workflow, and taking greater initiative in group technical reviews.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The culture emphasizes continuous learning and mutual respect. I particularly valued the blameless problem-solving mindset across the community, where technical mistakes were treated as learning opportunities rather than faults. Even as an intern, I felt fully integrated into the technical discussions and community activities.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The program provided excellent resources, including access to official AWS labs, workshop events, and flexible scheduling that allowed me to balance university commitments alongside project deliverables effectively.

### Additional Questions

- **What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**  
  The most rewarding experience was successfully deploying a complete end-to-end IoT cloud architecture—connecting physical hardware via MQTT to AWS IoT Core, processing data with AWS Lambda, and delivering a live frontend on AWS Amplify. Seeing the full pipeline run smoothly in production was extremely satisfying.

- **What do you think the company should improve for future interns?**  
  Establishing a standardized onboarding checklist for cloud toolings and introducing weekly structured lightning-talk sessions would help new interns build confidence in public technical speaking and proactive reporting earlier in their journey.

- **If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they intern here? Why or why not?**  
  Yes, absolutely. FCAJ offers a rare combination of hands-on cloud exposure, real system deployment opportunities, and strong mentorship that genuinely prepares students to transition into industry-ready engineers.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

- **Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?**  
  Organizing a dedicated mid-term peer review or cross-team demo day would be a great addition. It would allow different teams to share their architectural trade-offs, code structures, and lessons learned with one another.

- **Would you like to continue this program in the future?**  
  Yes, I would welcome the opportunity to stay involved, either by contributing to advanced AWS project stages or helping mentor the next cohort as an AWS Student Builder.

- **Any other comments (free sharing):**  
  I am deeply grateful to the FCAJ organizers, mentors, and fellow builders for creating such an inspiring learning environment. This experience clarified my career roadmap in cloud engineering and provided practical skills I will carry forward.
